# LocalLabel privacy policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

LocalLabel is a Chrome extension for annotating images and videos. It is designed so that your data never leaves your computer.

**What the extension accesses**

- The images, videos and model files you choose to open, read directly from your disk through Chrome's file picker.
- If you click "Save to folder" or export into the folder, it writes annotation files into the folder you opened.

**What the extension stores**

- Your projects (labels, annotations, settings) and the folder permission you granted, in the extension's own IndexedDB storage inside your Chrome profile.
- You can delete a project at any time from the "Recent projects" list, or remove all data by uninstalling the extension.

**What the extension does not do**

- It makes no network requests. There is no server, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no account.
- It does not read web pages, browsing history, or any file you did not choose.
- It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.

**Contact**

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's repository or email the publisher listed on the Chrome Web Store page.
